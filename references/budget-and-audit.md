# Budget and audit

## Define scope first

State whether the quoted budget is per person or for the party and whether it includes intercity transport. If unclear, calculate both interpretations when the difference changes feasibility.

Track intercity transport, vehicle rental or charter, fuel, tolls, parking, lodging, breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, drinks, tickets, local transit, taxis, entertainment, shopping, insurance, and contingency. Show daily totals, category totals, per-person total, party total, and remaining buffer.

Use observed prices with retrieval dates when available. Mark future lodging, airfare, train, and rental rates as volatile. Do not replace missing prices with false precision; use a labeled range.

## Recalculate after scope changes

Adding a city or region affects transport, nights, meals, tickets, vehicle days, fuel, tolls, and contingency. Recompute all of them. Do not append the new cost to an obsolete total.

## If the plan exceeds budget

Preserve MUST items and optimize in this order:

1. remove unnecessary taxis, transfers, or vehicle days;
2. change lodging area or tier while protecting safety and sleep;
3. remove low-value paid attractions;
4. replace expensive trend restaurants with strong local options;
5. shorten remote detours or use a better open-jaw transport pairing;
6. reduce optional cafés, desserts, and shopping.

If it still does not fit, provide both:

- the comfortable recommendation with a realistic cost range;
- an executable compressed version within or closest to budget, with explicit omissions and risks.

## Audit checklist

Check and repair:

- every MUST appears and its access rules are verified;
- no event overlaps or violates opening, last entry, or daily limits;
- meals occur at reasonable times and have nearby backups;
- city days avoid needless cross-city movement;
- regional routes minimize long repeated corridors;
- long transfer days include rest, fuel or charging, toilets, and daylight safety;
- lodging sequence follows the route and avoids needless one-night moves;
- walking, altitude, heat, cold, rain, and driving match the traveler;
- budget includes every category in scope and retains contingency;
- Plan B is executable rather than a list of indoor ideas;
- volatile facts have refresh dates and unresolved facts say `【待确认】`.

Run the script, then perform the geographic and freshness review manually.
